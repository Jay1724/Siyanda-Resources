Our Focus Areas

Our areas of interest are in Platinum Group Metals (PGMs), Chrome, Base Metals, Energy and Battery Metals. All the projects acquired are on a long term basis, with the optimisation of assets being the key goal.

Chrome

RST Special Metal (Pty) Ltd (“RST”) operates furnaces that process industrial waste material into various metal products that can be re-used in the manufacturing process including chrome and other non-ferrous metals.

Platinum Group Metals

Siyanda Bakgatla Platinum Mine (SBPM) is a rapidly growing, non-listed black-owned emerging Platinum Group Metal (PGM) mining and processing business.

Smelting

The strategic imperative of the chrome division is to be the leader in UG2 chrome extraction through the processing and production of low-cost chrome from UG Tailings source.

Energy

In 2012 Power Alt reached a milestone to become one of the first gas Independent Power Producers to connect and sell power to the SA state utility; Eskom.
